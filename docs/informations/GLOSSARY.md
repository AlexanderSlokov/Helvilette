# Glossary

## Naughtian

Naughtian is actually came from the naming of a character in my saga. 
She is the goddess of "the end", "death", and titled with "God-mother of Destruction".

## Helvilette

Helvilette is the naming of a character in my saga, pronounced as 'hel-vih-let'.
Helvilette is Naught's cat (litterally. I have a black cat named "Helvilette the First" too!).
He is the god who control the cycle of life and death, a Psychopomp, usually take the shape of a black cat with blue eyes.
His Agents is operating in all Realms, which made he had to oganize them in a living-k8s-alike system.

## Helvilette Othela

One of twelve long-term-operating Agents of Helvilette, act as the apiserver for his living-k8s-alike system.
In this project, Othela is the control plane (which included `helvilette-api-server`, `helvilette-exec-manager`, `helvilette-controller-manager`).
